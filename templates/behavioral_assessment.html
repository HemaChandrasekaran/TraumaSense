Behavioral Assessment

Please answer the following questions based on how you have been feeling recently.

1. How difficult is it for you to concentrate on your usual activities right now?

2. How often have you felt nervous, restless, or unable to relax recently?

3. When you face a stressful situation, how easy is it for you to make decisions?

4. How comfortable do you feel asking someone for help when you need it?

5. How much has stress affected your sleep or daily routine?



This assessment provides a supporting behavioral indicator and is not a medical diagnosis.
