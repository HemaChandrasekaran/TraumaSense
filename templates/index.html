Informed Consent

This prototype uses AI to analyze the information you provide and generate an AI-assisted stress and vulnerability assessment.

The assessment is not a medical or psychological diagnosis.

AI results should be reviewed by authorized human personnel before support or intervention decisions are made.

🔒 Privacy & Confidentiality

Information submitted through this prototype should be treated as confidential.

This prototype should not be used with real victim information without appropriate security, legal, ethical and organizational approval.
